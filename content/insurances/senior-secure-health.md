## Built for senior citizens

Standard health plans become expensive and restrictive with age. **Senior
Secure Health** is tailored for parents and older adults, with benefits that
matter at that stage of life.

## What stands out

- **Entry age up to 75 years**, with lifetime renewability.
- **Domiciliary treatment** — hospital care at home when admission is not
  possible.
- **Ambulance charges** reimbursed.
- **Annual health check-up** to catch issues early.

## Please note

- Pre-existing conditions have a waiting period, typically 3–4 years.
- Premiums rise with age and are higher than younger-age plans.
- A pre-policy medical examination may be required above a certain age.
