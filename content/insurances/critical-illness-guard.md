## Why a critical illness plan?

A hospitalisation policy reimburses medical bills. A **critical illness plan**
instead pays a **lump sum** when you are diagnosed with a covered condition,
helping replace lost income, pay for treatment or cover loan EMIs.

## How it works

1. You are diagnosed with a covered illness.
2. You submit the diagnosis and supporting documents.
3. On approval, the sum insured is paid directly to you.

## Good to know

- This is a **defined-benefit** plan and works alongside, not instead of, a
  health insurance policy.
- There is an initial waiting period for most illnesses.
- Survival period clauses may apply for certain conditions.
