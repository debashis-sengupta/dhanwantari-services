## One cover for the whole family

A **family floater** pools a single sum insured across all insured members. It
is usually far more economical than buying separate individual policies,
especially for young families.

## Highlights

- Cover spouse, up to 3 children and dependent parents under one policy.
- **Maternity benefit** after the applicable waiting period, including
  new-born cover.
- **Unlimited restoration** of the sum insured during the policy year.
- No pre-policy medical tests for members up to 45 years (subject to
  declarations).

## Things to consider

- The shared sum insured can be exhausted if one member makes a large claim.
- Adding elderly parents may attract a higher premium and waiting periods.
- Disclose all pre-existing conditions to avoid claim rejection.

> Talk to me before adding parents to a floater — for some ages an individual
> senior-citizen plan works out better.
