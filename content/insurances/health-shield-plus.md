## Who is this plan for?

**Health Shield Plus** is designed for working professionals and their
dependents who want strong hospitalisation cover with minimal out-of-pocket
expenses. It is ideal if you value a wide cashless hospital network and do not
want to worry about room-rent sub-limits.

## Key benefits

- **Sum insured up to ₹10 Lakh** with an option to increase at renewal.
- **Automatic restoration** of the sum insured once per policy year for
  unrelated illnesses.
- **Pre and post hospitalisation** expenses covered for 60 and 90 days
  respectively.
- **Day-care procedures** covered without the need for 24-hour admission.
- **Annual health check-up** for all insured members.

## What is not covered

- Pre-existing diseases during the initial waiting period (typically 3 years).
- Cosmetic treatments unless medically necessary due to an accident.
- Self-inflicted injuries and non-medical consumables as per the policy.
