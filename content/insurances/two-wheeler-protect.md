## Smart cover for your two-wheeler

Whether it is your daily commuter or a weekend bike, **Two-Wheeler Protect**
keeps you covered against accidents, theft and third-party liability at an
affordable premium.

## What is covered

- **Accident damage** to your vehicle.
- **Theft, fire and natural calamities.**
- **Third-party liability** — mandatory under the Motor Vehicles Act.
- Optional **helmet cover** and **pillion rider** cover.

## Why buy comprehensive instead of third-party only?

Third-party cover protects others but not your own vehicle. Comprehensive cover
protects both, and the extra cost is small compared to the cost of repairs or
replacing a stolen bike.
