## Complete protection for your car

**Comprehensive Car Shield** covers both third-party liability (mandatory by
law) and damage to your own vehicle. It is the recommended choice for any car
you care about.

## Included in this plan

- **Own-damage cover** for accidents, theft, fire, natural disasters and
  vandalism.
- **Third-party liability** for injury or property damage caused to others.
- **Zero depreciation** — no deduction for depreciation on replaced parts.
- **Roadside assistance** for breakdowns, flat tyres and towing.
- **Engine & gearbox protection** for water-related damage (recommended for
  monsoon-prone cities).

## Add-ons you can choose

- Consumables cover (engine oil, coolant, nuts and bolts).
- Return-to-invoice cover for total loss or theft.
- Key replacement and personal belongings cover.
