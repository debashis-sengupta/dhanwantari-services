## The minimum cover required by law

Every vehicle on Indian roads must have at least **third-party liability
insurance** under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988. This plan meets that
requirement.

## What it covers

- Injury, disability or death of a third party, up to the prescribed limits.
- Damage to a third party's property.
- **Personal accident cover** for the owner-driver, as mandated.

## What it does **not** cover

- Damage to your own vehicle.
- Theft of your own vehicle.
